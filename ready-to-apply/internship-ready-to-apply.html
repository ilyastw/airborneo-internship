Ready to Apply?

Here's what you need to know

Internship Criteria

  • Commit to a minimum internship period of 6 months
  • Maintain a minimum CGPA of 3.00 and above
  • Be actively enrolled in a Diploma, Bachelor's, Master's Degree, or equivalent programme

Application Checklist

  • Official internship request letter from your university or polytechnic
  • Reply form from your institution
  • Updated resume and cover letter
  • Academic certificates and transcripts, including latest CGPA

How to Apply

Interested Sarawakian graduates can apply through the official SCOPE portal at
gets-programs.netlify.app

Be sure to select AirBorneo Holdings as your preferred placement organization.

Please note: Accommodation, transportation and meals are not provided. An internship allowance will be offered.
