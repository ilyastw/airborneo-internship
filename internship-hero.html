Internship Programme At
AirBorneo

At AirBorneo, our Internship Programme is more than just work experience – it's an opportunity to contribute meaningfully, gain industry insights, and develop practical skills in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

  • • On-the-job training with real responsibilities and mentorship
  • • Professional development workshops curated by industry experts
  • • Placement in dynamic organizations like AirBorneo, where innovation meets purpose

Partnership with SCOPE Sarawak

AirBorneo is a proud partner of SCOPE Sarawak (Sarawak Centre of Performance Excellence), working collaboratively to support and host fresh graduate placements under the GETS–Work Readiness Programme (GETS-WRP).


Through this partnership, we contribute to developing Sarawakian talent by offering real-world exposure, mentorship, and meaningful roles within our organization.
